The real CPF, month by month

Contributions, allocations and interest follow CPF's own 2026 rules for every age, worked out monthly the way the CPF Board does it.

  • Every age band. Rates change at 35, 45, 50, 55, 60, 65 and 70, on wages up to the $8,000 ceiling.
  • Extra interest and caps. 1% more on your first $60,000 (up to 2% from 55), and MediSave above the $79,000 BHS flows on to your other accounts.
  • Top ups that stop where CPF stops them. Your SA up to the FRS, your RA up to the ERS, with tax relief and matching where they apply.

Buy a home the Singapore way

Live with your parents, rent, or buy. The game sizes your loan and grants the way HDB and the banks would.

  • BTO or resale. Enhanced Housing Grant up to $120,000, plus resale grants, by household income.
  • HDB or bank loans. Capped by the 30% MSR and 55% TDSR, then paid from your OA, cash, or both.
  • Later in life. Accrued interest when you sell, Voluntary Housing Refunds, the Lease Buyback Scheme and the Silver Housing Bonus.

Your life, told back to you

There is no score to beat. When you reach 85, the game tells your story: your career, your homes, your CPF decisions and how you chose to live.

  • Questions, not grades. Were you satisfied? What would you change? Good for a class discussion.
  • Rewind. Go back a few turns and see how a different choice plays out.

Your animal family

Play as a capybara, cat, unicorn, dog or frog. Your partner and children are the same animal, with their ages over their heads.

Life happens

Illnesses paid through subsidies, MediShield Life, MediSave and Shield plans. Recessions, AI at work, scams, and family surprises.

Money beyond CPF

Cash savings, credit card debt at 25% a year, cars with a 10 year COE, and investing once or every year, with cash or through CPFIS.

FAQ

Before you start

Is it really free?

Yes. It runs in your browser with no download, no account and no ads.

Who is it for?

Students meeting CPF for the first time, and adults who want to see how today's choices play out by retirement. It works well in a classroom: everyone plays a life, then compares their recaps.

Are the numbers real?

The CPF, HDB, MAS and MediShield Life figures are the official 2026 ones. Prices, pay and investment returns are realistic estimates, kept in today's dollars. Companies and funds in the game are made up.

Does it save my game?

After every turn, in the browser you are playing on. Pick it up later with Continue. Clearing your browser's site data erases the save.

Can I play on my phone?

Yes. The layout works on phones, tablets and desktops.

Why animals?

So anyone can see themselves in the game. Pick the family you like best.
